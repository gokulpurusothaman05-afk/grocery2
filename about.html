Our Heritage

Stackly Sourcing

Founded in 2020, Stackly arose from a simple realization: finding verified organic fruits, fresh nuts, and pure bakery goods was far too complex. We set out to bypass massive distributors and work directly with individual farmers.

Today, we supply fresh berry baskets, raw vegetable juices, and natural foods to thousands of health-focused households daily, maintaining a strict cold-chain logistics operation.

Our Core Farm Values

Eco-Sustainability

Utilizing bio-composting and crop-rotation methods to keep farm soils nutrient-rich naturally.
