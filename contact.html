How fast are baskets delivered?

We deliver within 24 hours of farm harvesting! Subscriptions are scheduled precisely to your preferred date and time.

Do you ship fresh juices outside NY/NJ?

To guarantee peak freshness and enzyme activity, cold pressed raw juices are restricted to our local NY & NJ logistics network.

We maintain a 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. Contact our billing department, and we will issue an immediate replacement or refund.
